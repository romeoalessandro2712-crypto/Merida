# MERIDIA — competitor analysis (November 2026)

Sources: the public pages of each site, fetched on 23 November 2026. Only what was visible in
those pages is reported. Promotions change often, so re-check before quoting.

## Nude Project (nude-project.com/it-eu)

**Not analysed.** The site answered automated requests with HTTP 429 (too many requests /
bot protection), so no content could be read. Review it manually in a browser.

## Notre Succès (notresucces.com/it)

At the time of fetching, the homepage was a **Black Week "waiting room"**: a countdown,
"Le porte si aprono tra 3 … Non chiudere questa pagina", then redirect to the offers.

What they do well
- Event-driven homepage that builds anticipation (countdown, timed doors).
- **Gift with purchase by spend tier**: free sweater from €150, free jacket from €350.
- **Members' club** newsletter ("Entra nel club Notre Succès"): early access and member offers.
- Deep navigation: In evidenza / Novità / Best seller + 11 categories; country, currency and language selectors.
- Service details: order tracking link, phone number and email, a withdrawal form (Modulo di recesso).
- Social: Instagram and TikTok.

Weaknesses / openings
- While the sale is gated, **no product can be browsed** from the homepage: discovery is blocked.
- Positioning is **discount-led** (up to −40% + gifts), which weakens a premium image.
- No reviews, brand story or editorial content visible on the fetched homepage.

## Âmedorée (amedoree.com)

What they do well
- **New-in carousel** right at the top; clear Men / Women split; "Shop by collection";
  "Back by Popular Demand".
- **Trust row** under the hero: Free shipping · Duties and taxes included · 14-day returns · Secure payment.
- Product cards show colour, gender and **regular vs. sale price with a % badge**.
- **Pre-order** for sold-out pieces with a ship date ("Ships around 11 December").
- Product page: **size guide** with a fit scale (Fitted / Regular / Relaxed), a measurement table
  (cm / in), how to measure, a ±2 cm tolerance note, and **model info** ("Model is 168 cm and wears XS").
- **Storytelling descriptions** (the meaning of the product name), composition, delivery times by region,
  a help search on the product page, "Complete the look" with inline size selection.
- **Social proof**: Trustpilot reviews with first name, country and date; a community section with
  Instagram handles; Membership, Track Your Order and Affiliate links.

Weaknesses / openings
- **Permanent markdowns** (−20% to −31% on most products) erode perceived value.
- Premium storytelling but **synthetic blends** (e.g. 30% nylon, 20% acrylic, 10% spandex on a "mohair" knit).
- No visible **origin / traceability** information (where fibres come from, where pieces are made).
- Pre-orders mean **uncertain waits** for the customer.
- The size guide is static: it doesn't **recommend** a size.

## Concentré (concentreclo.com)

What they do well
- **Rating in the header** ("Rated 4.5/5 by our customers") and a reviews row with titled reviews
  ("Top quality, no logo markup", "Designer quality").
- **Clear value message**: designer-level quality "without overpaying for the logo".
- **Founder voice**: co-founder quotes about the brand's purpose, mixed with customer quotes.
- Product page: **"Find My Size"** (height, weight, preferred fit), model info with height, weight
  and size ("Model is 180 cm tall, weighs 70 kg and wears size M"), "You're saving €50",
  social proof ("Vincenzo, Niklas and 1797 others love Concentré").
- **FAQ on the homepage** and product page; an online **withdrawal (return) form**.

Weaknesses / openings
- **Slow delivery**: "6–10 business days" (FAQ) and "6–8 business days" (product page); a review
  mentions long shipping.
- **Customers pay for returns** ("You bear the direct cost of returning the goods").
- Heavy discounting (Black Friday up to −40%, most products shown with crossed-out prices).

## What MERIDIA implemented

Borrowed from what works
- Size guide with fit scale, measurements in cm/in, how to measure, tolerance note and model info.
- Real delivery dates on product, cart and checkout (business days, 14:00 dispatch cut-off).
- Trust row on the product page (free shipping, EU duties included, free returns, secure payment).
- Story behind each product name.
- Members' club framing for the newsletter (early access, private sales, birthday gift).
- Order tracking page and link from the order confirmation.

Opportunities neither competitor covers
- **Size finder**: height + usual size + fit preference → a recommended size you can select in one tap.
- **Remembered size**: the size you chose last is pre-selected on the next product.
- **Full traceability card** on every product: fibre origin, where it's made, composition
  ("no synthetic fibres" where true).
- **"Notify me" instead of pre-orders** for sold-out sizes: no uncertain waits.
- **Rewards instead of permanent markdowns**: prices stay honest; savings come from the multi-piece
  discount or the welcome gift, and never stack.
- **No gated homepage**: the scratch card and offers never block browsing.
- **Free returns with an instant label** (online return request), where Concentré charges for returns.
- **Faster, dated delivery**: 2–4 working days with real dates, versus 6–10 days.
- **Pieces shown worn**: illustrated figures on product cards, in the gallery and in a shoppable
  lookbook with hotspots, plus a weight-aware size finder and an animated brand manifesto.
- **Smoother checkout**: step indicator, express payment, live validation, card formatting,
  "remember my details", delivery dates in the summary, faster page transitions.

Note: origin, mill locations and model measurements in MERIDIA's product data are placeholder
content for this concept and must be replaced with real supplier information before any launch.
